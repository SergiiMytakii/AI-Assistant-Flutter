Follow these recomendations to have a better accuracy of AI accistant responces

  • Questions and Answers in your knowledge dataset have to be unique:
     By enforcing uniqueness, we ensure that each question has a distinct meaning and is associated with the most appropriate answer, enhancing the overall effectiveness and reliability of the AI assistant.
  • Cover all hypothetical questions of your clients:
    It's crucial to address a wide range of potential questions that clients may ask. By covering hypothetical questions, we ensure that the AI assistant is equipped to provide comprehensive and relevant responses, enhancing the user experience. You can download some examples:
    Example for hotels
    Example for apartment hosts
    Example for hostels
    Feel free to use the examples as a template for your own questions and answers.
  • Provide detailed responses:
     It's important to offer comprehensive answers that thoroughly address the user's query. For example, instead of simply stating "Yes," the AI assistant could respond with additional context or explanations to ensure the user fully understands the information provided.
  • Provide some information about your company:
     It's important to offer users basic details about your company, such as its name, address, and key offerings.
  • Choose from what language AI assistant will start the conversation:
     In Settings, you can select the language in which the AI assistant will engage with users initially. This allows users to communicate comfortably in their preferred language right from the start, facilitating smoother interactions and ensuring effective communication.
      Note: You can choose up to 2 languages.
      Note: If the user begins speaking in another language, the AI assistant will reply in the user's chosen language.
  • Enable the generation of rephrased questions in Settings:
     This feature allows users to activate the generation of alternative phrasings for questions within the Settings menu. For example, enabling this option could provide users with varied ways to ask a question, improving the AI assistant's ability to understand and respond effectively to diverse user inputs.
